Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you play Overheard, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication data provided by Google (if using Google Login).
  • Gameplay Data: We store your daily puzzle results (guesses used and clues revealed) to provide you with historical statistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the game.
  • Securely authenticate your account and persist your game progress across devices.
  • Generate anonymous global statistics (e.g., "15 people solved Overheard today").

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using Supabase. Passwords are cryptographically hashed, and we employ Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that your gameplay statistics can only be accessed by you.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services like Google (for authentication) and Vercel (for hosting and basic analytics). These services may collect basic telemetry data as outlined in their respective privacy policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at overheardsupport@gmail.com.
